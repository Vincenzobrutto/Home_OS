# Copilot Round 4 — Workflow benchmark intake

**Date:** 2026-10-12. **Source:** 17-page PDF uploaded in conversation, `dimora_v3_ricerca_indipendente_workflow_professionali_dimora_home_20261012_134805.pdf`. **Status:** research, not roadmap or implementation approval.

## Findings
- **ImmoDoc** publicly claims checklist, granular participants, missing docs, professional remediation, persistent property record and buyer handover. Handover is not novel. Dimora would have to win on actual UX/work reduction and more structured continuing owner record (asset/intervention/warranty/common/private), not on feature absence.
- **Faloosa** documents assisted AI/ML document intake and multi-party workflows; **Gestim** has customizable workflows/due-diligence at Platinum tier. Comparison must be against working alternatives.
- **Danea Domustudio**, **Multidomus**, **GeCoMAX**, **Maintego** already support substantial shared assets, maintenance, documents and owner portals. Condo-only generic dashboard has weak independent premium value.
- **Five candidate improvements:** guided mobile intake without friction; readiness with states + provenance; selective collaborator access; conversion of documents into durable house entities; controlled copy-on-handover.
- **Most plausible pricing test:** per completed practice; free partner + paid Gift as comparison; subscription with included Gift only after regular use is observed.
- Verified competitors' public prices do not establish Dimora WTP. No proven time savings, AI accuracy superiority, owner activation/retention, or CRM/condo API integrations.

## Implications / needed tests
1. Find measurable performance differences vs agency's actual workflow and competitor product, including double entry and active staff time.
2. Test real paid commitments before any workflow engineering.
3. The output to Home should be an authorized and selectively copied permanent property record, not automatically transferred sensitive practice files.
4. Owner Home remains one experience with My Property, Common Parts where authorized, and private personal documentation.
5. No change to ADR #77, feature roadmap or code. Await Claude Round 4 architecture report and synthesize V4 as a validated-prototype candidate.
