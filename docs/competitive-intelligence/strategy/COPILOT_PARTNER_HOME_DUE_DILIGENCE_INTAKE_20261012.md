# Copilot due diligence — Dimora Partner + Home (12 October 2026)
Status: research intake; 15-page PDF supplied in chat `due_diligence_indipendente_dimora_partner_dimora_home_20261012_122520.pdf`. No roadmap, ADR or code decisions.

## Verdict
- **Agency first, TEST not product GO**: Transaction Readiness Workspace (guided seller intake, AI document classification+human confirmation, present/missing/verify status, selective exports and eventual controlled handover) creating pre-populated owner Home. Strong incumbent overlap (Gestim, Faloosa and ImmoDoc).
- **Gift**: TEST; should be outcome of a professional process, not empty promotional account. No proof of WTP, owner activation or renewal.
- **Administrator**: weak as software/Gift payer, potential referral/channel or authorized documentation provider; existing Danea, Multidomus, Maintego cover much. Do not assume condo funds pay private Gifts.
- Alpha supports document-to-asset/intervention/guarantee, confirmation, timeline, provenance. Partner tenancy/RBAC, buyer handover, collaborative permissions, paid Gift, CRM integration are **NOT** already operational. Existing authentication docs have conflicting age/version statements.

## Copilot business model vs Gemini Round 2
**Copilot test first:** free Partner Lite, paid per completed practice (trial pricing **€49/€99/€149** speculative), concierge test 5 agencies x 3 practices, paid commitments, time measurements, 30-day owner activation. Budget illustrative €4–12k for external concierge / legal / support, **not** software development. Administrator owner-driven handover only later.
**Gemini Round 2:** paid Partner Lite **€59/month** with **12 Gifts/year** (pure scenario, unverified) and B2B recurring central.
**Convergence:** Agency utility independent of Gift; pre-populated, user-controlled Home is differentiation; no replacement CRM/condo ERP; don't build full Partner now.
**Disagreement to resolve:** pay-per-practice vs subscription bundle; first value job; whom agency charges / how many real cases; economics of access/transfer; administrator as distributor should not be prematurely excluded just because cannot be payer.

## Competitive caveats
- Faloosa offers guided collection/AI/ML, professional participation and dossier. ImmoDoc offers dossier + buyer transfer. No absolute novelty claim.
- Danea and Multidomus have substantive common assets, technical maintenance and workflows.
- International HomeZada PRO Gift $99/property publicly quoted; HomeBinder/Chimni/hop analogues, but conversion/retention not independently documented.
- Product features existing elsewhere may nonetheless be attractive **if Dimora measurably improves UX, speed, total effort or linkage to owner record**. Avoid false tests based on 'unique' alone.

## Research weaknesses
- Paid buyer willingness still unknown; 5x3 pilots need actual practices and consent.
- Cost and price range not observed demand.
- Heavy permissions, data provenance and transfer not trivial; seller data cannot automatically pass to buyer, condo docs are not universally public. Avoid 'certified', 'verified' and compliance conclusions.
- The report's funnel formula uses an `a` activation factor for Gift revenues that may conflate **sold** Gift with **redeemed** Gift; reconcile unit of sale and recognition.

## Claude red-team tasks
1. Independently adjudicate agency vs admin as **distribution channel**, separately from **software payer**.
2. Challenge realistic 3-way commercial comparison: (A) Gift-only pay-per-instance; (B) free Lite + paid Gift/practice; (C) paid Lite subscription incl. Gift; optional (D) pro later.
3. Compare partner workflow ROI on a current CRM/Faloosa/ImmoDoc workflow and measure **extra steps**, not novelty.
4. Separate professional record (seller/practice) from owner long-lived home record and model explicit handover/permissions and no default access.
5. Sensitivity on number of practices per agent, win rate, gift paid vs activated vs renewed, assisted onboarding, support/CAC, long-term owner engagement.
6. Explicit GO TEST / HOLD / NO GO gates for each role and commercial option.
7. Don't modify roadmap; draft research only.

Current owner direction: **new functions allowed even if absent from alpha**; strategic restriction is avoid build until evidence. Unique market functionality **not required**, provided demonstrable preferred functionality.
