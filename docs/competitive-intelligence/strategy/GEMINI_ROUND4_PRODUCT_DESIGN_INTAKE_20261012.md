# Gemini Round 4 — Product Design intake
Date: 2026-10-12. Source: user-provided Gemini Product Design text, 186 lines, in conversation.
Status: research, not accepted product specification. Roadmap, ADR, code unchanged.

## Proposed user journeys
Agency: create dossier, mass upload and AI split-screen human validation, checklist, selective handover Gift. Administrator: building setup, common docs and dates, owner-sharing 1:N. Owner: one Home incorporating private house and common areas, selection for future ownership change.

## Valuable ideas
- Document on left / extracted fields on right, confirmation and legibility errors.
- Seller/technician document collection links.
- Indexed PDF/ZIP dossier to produce utility before Gift.
- Administrative import to avoid double entry only if actual exports/APIs are usable.
- Building emergency contact directory as possible smaller owner value (needs interview).

## Errors/risks
1. Gemini introduction treats collection/Gift as if in current alpha; professional flow, Gift, invite, handover do not yet exist.
2. Red/yellow/green *conformity* status is misleading. Show documentary readiness (present/missing/to verify) only; no certified claims.
3. 'Opt-out' copying real estate docs and auto-granting condo documents are not safe defaults; needs owner authorization, seller-led selection, recipient access and condo publication rights.
4. Agent-only checkbox 'I removed personal ID' inadequate privacy filtering; structured selection/review and audit; copy-on-handover rather than wholesale practice transfer.
5. Permanently displaying agent brand in private Home could impede independence; limit to disclosed opt-in sponsorship period, test retention.
6. Co-owners need robust account permissions; cannot be assumed present.
7. Proposal of giving 5 free Gifts to 10 agencies tests activation but not B2B WTP, so add paid commitment or quote/deposit before concierge; do not claim successful economics from free giveaway.
8. Proposed integrations to Danea via CSV/XML are unverified; developing plugin before evidence contradicts no-code learning aim.
9. Automatic building↔unit matching by postal/cadastral details and email is risky; owner-verified claim with authorized invitation only.
10. AI-based deadline/reminder should show source, applicability and human verification; do not promise compliance.

## Decision pending
Await Copilot workflow benchmark and Claude architecture review. Then synthesize a V4 with owner Home unified, agency utility, admin utility, and selective handover. Compare no-code paid tests (agency doc dossier vs practitioner maintenance) before development.
