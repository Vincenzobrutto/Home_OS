# Gemini Partner Round 2 — review

Date: 2026-10-12. Research hypothesis only; no roadmap or code changes.

Gemini recommends paid Partner Lite for agencies (€59/month with 12 annual Gifts) with three features: AI document triage, technical export, and owner handover. Condo track: common assets/deadlines, supplier contracts, and a building document hub. This is not validated buyer willingness to pay.

Important corrections:
- ImmoDoc official site claims buyer transfer at closing and agency branding: https://www.immodoc.it/fascicolo-di-compravendita . Gemini claim of no Italian competitor offering handover is incorrect.
- Faloosa offers AI-assisted data/document collection, not only static checklists: https://www.faloosa.it/ .
- Danea Domustudio manages common-parts contracts, maintenance, deadlines and interventions: https://www.danea.it/software/domustudio/funzionalita/ .
- Gemini break-even: yearly agency contribution €459.70 before CAC, €109.70 after assumed €350 CAC. Recovering €18,000 first-year development requires ~164 newly acquired agencies at €109.70 contribution, not 39. 39 is steady-state before CAC and overhead. Other prices, activation rates and spending estimates are illustrative, not observed data.
- Agent document handover, condo publication and roles need authorization and a permission model. No automatic transfer, no certified/conformity claim.

Conclusion: compare paid Lite + included Gifts against pay-per-Gift and free Lite, with real buyer commitments and actual cost of professional workflow. Await independent Copilot report before Claude red team. No product decision made.
