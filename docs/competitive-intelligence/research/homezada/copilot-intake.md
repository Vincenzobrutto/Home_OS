2. Professional Gift/Transfer pricing stated as **$99 per property one-off**, Share as **$49/property/year**; FAQ discrepancy of $25 vs $49 identified (p. 10).
3. Consumer Essentials free, Premium **$99/year** or **$15.95/month**, Deluxe **$189/year** (pp. 9–10).

## Preliminary Dimora implications (hypotheses, not product decisions)
- Agency workflow must deliver *immediate operational value*, not merely branded marketing after closing.
- Differentiate provider-controlled initial setup from owner-controlled private record after handover.
- Minimize setup burden; define activation and repeat-usage experiments before inferring retention.
- Model professional billing per case/transfer vs recurring share; willingness-to-pay in Italy not established.
- Builder handover is an adjacent channel worth exploring, not yet in committed distribution scope.
