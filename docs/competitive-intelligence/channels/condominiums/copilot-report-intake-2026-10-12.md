## Executive synthesis from the uploaded report
- No demonstrated defensible standalone wedge in traditional condominium software; ledgers, communications, tickets, documents and portals are already covered.
- Leading *pilot hypothesis*: `Building Events → Property Timeline`, translating confirmed works / building events to each affected unit's persistent history, strictly separating common building records from private owner records.
- Alternative use cases: sale/change-of-ownership handover; anagrafe as opt-in onboarding only, not a standalone product.
- Suggested channel hypothesis: administrator as referral/originator, **not necessarily paying buyer**.
- Key constraints: zero duplicated entry, minimal administrative effort, controlled owner-only vault, provenance, optional/legally grounded data sharing, interoperability with incumbent software.

## Pilot ideas mentioned (author proposals, not commitments)
- 5 studios / 20 condos / 50 authentic documents, measure event extraction quality, corrections and owner activations.
- 20 sales to evaluate transaction handover.
- 200-unit anagrafe update to measure optional owner opt-in.

## Red-team issues
- Weak recurring owner need; incumbent TeamSystem/Danea feature replication; privacy/ownership of records; cost of integrations; lack of validated willingness to pay.
- A critical unresolved dependency is verifying available APIs and permitted data flows with Domustudio Cloud Pro.
- Need external legal review for privacy roles and transfer to a new owner; vendor-supported claims require independent checks.
